Budget Justification

Senior personnel: the PI requests three summer months per year at an annual base salary of $95,000. Co-PI Dr. Nguyen requests 0.5 summer months per year.

Other personnel: two graduate research assistants at 12 months each, at $28,000 per year plus tuition remission.

Equipment: a Gamry Interface 1010E potentiostat, $18,400, required for the impedance measurements in Aim 2.

Travel: one domestic conference per year for the PI and one student ($2,400/yr), plus the annual NSF grantee meeting in Washington, DC ($1,200/yr).

Participant support: fifteen high school participants in the summer module, at $500 stipend each. We also budget $1,200 in incentive payments to research subjects under participant support to improve retention.

Other direct costs: we request $600 for wine and beer for the annual project reception, and $900 for catered lunches and coffee breaks at our internal monthly team meetings. We also request $3,600 per year to rent home office workspace for the PI. Local participants attending the Baltimore workshop will receive per diem of $75 per day.

Materials and supplies: monomers, chain transfer agents, sensor housings and consumables, $18,000 per year.

Indirect costs are calculated at Morgan State's federally negotiated rate of 51% of modified total direct costs. The university will contribute $25,000 of voluntary committed cost sharing to demonstrate institutional support.
