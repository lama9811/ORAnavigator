Budget Justification

The organization defines a project year as a twelve-month period beginning on the award start date, not the academic year.

Senior personnel. The PI, Dr. A. Researcher, requests two summer months per year at an annual institutional base salary of $95,000, for $15,833 per year. The Co-PI, Dr. M. Nguyen, requests one summer month per year at an annual base salary of $88,000, for $7,333 per year. Both allocations support synthesis oversight, model development, and supervision of the field deployment campaign described in the Project Description.

Other personnel. No postdoctoral associates or other professional personnel are requested. Two graduate research assistants are requested at $32,000 per year each, supporting polymer synthesis and impedance characterization. Four undergraduate researchers are requested at 10 hours per week for 30 weeks at $17.00 per hour, for $5,100 each. No secretarial or clerical personnel are requested.

Equipment. A Gamry Interface 1010E potentiostat is requested at $18,400 in Year 1. Impedance spectroscopy across the 0-35 PSU range is the central measurement of Aim 2, and the department's existing instrument is committed to another award for the full project period.

Travel. Domestic travel of $2,400 per year supports two people attending one national meeting to present results, plus $1,200 per year for the PI to attend the annual NSF grantee meeting in Washington, DC. Field travel of $1,800 per year covers eleven round trips to the two Chesapeake Bay deployment stations at the federal mileage rate.

Materials and supplies. $9,200 per year covers zwitterionic monomers, initiators, dialysis membranes, reference electrodes, and sensor housings. Estimates are based on current vendor quotations retained on file.

Other direct costs. $1,400 per year covers the institutional core facility rate for NMR time at $45 per hour, billed at the same rate charged to all internal users.

Indirect costs. Facilities and administrative costs are calculated at Morgan State University's negotiated on-campus organized research rate of 54% of modified total direct costs, excluding equipment and the portion of each subaward beyond the first $25,000. No subawards are proposed.
